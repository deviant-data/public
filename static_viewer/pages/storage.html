What this shows

Total used, total remaining, daily contribution, projected longevity, archive coverage, restore status, and index counts.

Method

Storage uses the materialized storage payload plus hourly operations rows. It compares live database size, disk availability, archive coverage, and index counts rather than estimating longevity from a single raw count.

How to read it

Healthy long runs keep active storage bounded, daily growth visible, and verified archives increasing.

Why this matters

Long-term cost stability depends on knowing what is growing, how fast, and what can safely move cold.

Long horizon value

This is the 10-20 year viability panel: hot data stays small, cold data stays restorable, and pressure is visible early.
