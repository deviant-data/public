What this shows

Current topic-like groups built from embedded items and their representative samples.

Method

Clusters are groups derived from embedding proximity. The lineage section uses daily entity indexes as a compact bridge between cluster/triple structure and named entities.

How to read it

Samples explain the local meaning of a label; size alone is not importance.

Why this matters

Clusters are the visible middle layer between raw text and rules. They make the corpus easier to inspect without flattening it into one list.

Long horizon value

Clusters become lineage candidates: topics can grow, split, merge, or fade.
