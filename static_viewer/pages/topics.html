What this shows

Central entities, topic drift, and the topic river together, while the overview keeps only the top visible entities.

Method

Topics uses bounded daily topic indexes plus central-entity ranks. The river is a compact daily volume proxy; it is meant to show direction and persistence before a richer historical replay is needed.

How to read it

Use the entity list to identify stable named hubs, then use the river to see whether nearby topic labels are emerging, shrinking, or moving.

Why this matters

Topics are easier to understand when central entities and drift live together instead of forcing comparison across several disconnected pages.

Long horizon value

This becomes the decade-scale atlas: topic bands show emergence and decay, while entities explain what those bands were about.
