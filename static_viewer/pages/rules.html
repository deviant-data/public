rules

What this shows

A wide field of recurring relationships, kept expansive at first so faint returns are not erased before they can gather shape.

Method

Rules preserve a broad relation field before compression. Support, confidence, lift, exposure state, and optional Bayesian score describe how often a relationship returns and how stable that return has become.

How to read it

State marks exposure: observed, exploratory, mature, or robust. Score describes recurrence; it is not a verdict.

Why this matters

The first task is perception. Let the corpus show what repeats, what drifts, and what begins to stabilize.

Long horizon value

Over time, returning rules become memory. Absent rules fade without being forced out too early.
