rules

What this shows

Repeated relationships preserved as rules, with belief scores where Bayesian evidence exists.

Method

Rules are co-occurrence relationships with support, confidence, lift, and optional Bayesian belief. The maturation section rolls those relationships into daily score and maturity indexes.

How to read it

A useful rule is one that matures through repeated evidence, not a one-time coincidence.

Why this matters

Rules should show whether a relationship is becoming durable, weakening, or simply appearing because of source volume.

Long horizon value

Rule persistence and half-life matter more than any single high-scoring snapshot.
