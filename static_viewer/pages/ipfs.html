What this shows

Encrypted archive cells published to IPFS, including CIDs, checksums, pin status, and restore-linked archive evidence.

Method

IPFS reads archive_manifest and archive_ipfs_cells only. It does not call Kubo during a page request; live pin checks remain an explicit diagnosis operation.

How to read it

A durable cell is verified, encrypted, pinned, and tied back to a restorable archive. A pinned CID without restore evidence is only a location.

Why this matters

IPFS helps only when it strengthens recoverability. This view separates public-looking availability from long-term preservation.

Long horizon value

Over 10-20 years this becomes the archive survival map: what exists, what is still pinned, and what would fail if one machine disappeared.
