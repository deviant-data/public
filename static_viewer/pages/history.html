What this shows

Replay frames, archive inventory, and the storage footprint of historical views.

Method

History stores compact replay frames and archives them by year. It answers what changed without forcing every current dashboard view to keep old active rows hot.

How to read it

Use replay to answer what changed, not just what exists now.

Why this matters

History turns transient dashboard states into inspectable evidence without forcing every view to stay live forever.

Long horizon value

History is the bridge between the live dashboard and decade-scale evidence.
