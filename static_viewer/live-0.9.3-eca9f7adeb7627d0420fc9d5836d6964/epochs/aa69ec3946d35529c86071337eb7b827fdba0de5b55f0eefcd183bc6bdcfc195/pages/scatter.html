About this view

What this shows

A compact projection of embedding neighborhoods across items and clusters.

Method

The 3D scatter plot projects embeddings into three principal directions. Rotation and cluster color expose neighborhood structure without requiring a full vector database or external visualization package.

How to read it

Treat terrain and density as the signal; individual points are exemplars.

Why this matters

The projection shows proximity before labels harden. It is useful when emerging topics do not yet have stable names.

Long horizon value

Over time this 3D scatter plot becomes a topic atlas: continents, splits, merges, and fade-outs.
